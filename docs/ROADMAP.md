## Milestones

1. **Onboarding and music identity.** Done.
2. **Discover, connections and safety screens, with clearly fictional sample people.** Done, still local.
   Includes block, report (with "may be under 18" as a reason) and privacy settings.
3. **Backend. Started.** Local accounts, saving, deletion, email check, password reset and chat are done. Still to do: real people seeing each other, a proper email sender, and hosting (Supabase cloud only if a lawyer confirms it, otherwise a server in Mongolia).
   Needs the decisions at the end of `SAFETY.md`, and `DATA.md`.
4. **Trust and safety operations.** Moderator queue, photo review, rate limits and legal review.
5. **Closed pilot** for invited adults 18+, with native-speaker copy review and testing on real low-cost Android phones.
6. **Authorized music integrations**, such as Spotify sign-in or catalog search, subject to their developer terms.
