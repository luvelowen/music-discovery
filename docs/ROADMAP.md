## Milestones

1. **Onboarding and music identity.** Done.
2. **Discover, connections and safety screens, with clearly fictional sample people.** Done, still local.
   Includes block, report (with "may be under 18" as a reason), privacy settings and a demo of the 18+ check.
3. **Supabase.** Accounts, database, Row Level Security, server-side checks and full account deletion.
   Needs the decisions at the end of `SAFETY.md`.
4. **Trust and safety operations.** Moderator queue, photo review, rate limits and legal review.
5. **Closed pilot** for students aged 18+ at one university, with native-speaker copy review and testing on real low-cost Android phones.
6. **Authorized music integrations**, such as Spotify sign-in or catalog search, subject to their developer terms.
7. **Younger students**, only as a separate group, after legal review and working moderation.
